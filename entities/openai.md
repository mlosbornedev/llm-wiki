# OpenAI

AI research company led by Sam Altman. Planning one of the largest IPOs in history, but with significant financial challenges.

## IPO Details
- **Expected valuation:** $1T+
- **Planned raise:** $60B+ (part of ~$170–195B combined with SpaceX and Anthropic)
- **Timeline:** Within 12 months (as of article date ~April 2026)

## Financial Distress
The IPO is less a celebration and more a funding round of last resort:

- **2026 projected loss:** $14 billion
- **2027 projected cash burn:** $57 billion annually
- **Path to profitability:** 2030 (if everything goes to plan)
- After raising $122B in largest private fundraise in tech history, has roughly 18–24 months of operating runway before needing to raise again

### Stargate Project Failure
- January 2025: Sam Altman announced Stargate, a $500B AI infrastructure project to build data centers across the US, at the White House with President Trump
- Banks and lenders refused to underwrite the buildout — looked at a company burning billions/year with no profitability until 2030 and said no
- OpenAI walked away from planned expansion of flagship Stargate campus in Abilene, Texas
- Total projected compute spending revised down from $1.4T to ~$600B
- Instead of owning data centers, now **renting capacity from AWS, Google Cloud, and others**
- The public equity markets (your 401(k)) are now the next door to knock on after debt markets declined

## The IPO is a Bailout
The article frames the IPO not as a validation of AI revolution but as a "funding round" — the public market is the "lender of last resort after every other source of capital has either been tapped or has declined."
