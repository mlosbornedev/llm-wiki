# Apple

Apple is building an agentic runtime embedded in iOS — not competing in the model race, but building the platform layer every AI winner must route through.

## Core Strategy
- **Not** playing the model race (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google)
- **Playing** the platform play — the layer every model must pass through
- Apple has never lost a platform play it entered late

## The Four Signals
1. **Siri overhaul** (WWDC June 8, 2026) — "The chatbot is the skin. The skeleton is far more consequential."
2. **MCP integration into iOS** — wiring the Model Context Protocol directly into the OS
3. **Google Gemini deal** — asymmetric terms favoring the model-controlling party
4. **1.5B+ iPhones** in pockets of highest-spending consumers — distribution already owned

## The Apple Lateness Pattern
Apple historically enters markets "late" (MP3 players, smartphones, tablets, smartwatches, wireless audio) and wins anyway because it plays platform, not product.

## Critical Window
- **WWDC:** June 8, 2026
- **Architecture ships:** Fall 2026
- **Positioning window:** 10 weeks from article date

## Key Question for Builders
Will you be positioned as a user of Apple's runtime layer, or find ways to build value Apple can't commoditize?
