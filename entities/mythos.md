# Mythos

Anthropic's next frontier model. Delayed.

## Why It's Delayed
Two reasons cited in the article:
1. **Security concerns** — unresolved security issues in the model
2. **Compute demands** — serving Mythos would require roughly **10x more compute** than existing infrastructure. Nobody has that compute globally yet.

The world remains **deeply power-constrained**. When the bottleneck on the next model isn't the research but the electricity and hardware, the strategic logic shifts: stop trying to win on model releases alone, and start winning on the layer that makes customers sticky regardless of which model sits underneath.

## Implication
This delay is part of what drives [[anthropic-platform-strategy]] — Anthropic can't compete on frontier model releases alone (Mythos keeps getting pushed), so they compete on the **persistent agent layer** instead. The model is the loss leader. The agent that holds your memory, context, and workflows is the actual product.
