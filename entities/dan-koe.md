# Dan Koe

Creator of the "future/proof" newsletter (letters.thedankoe.com). Covers AI, creativity, one-person businesses, and human optimization for the modern creator economy.

## Key Positions

- **AI as creativity amplifier, not replacement** — AI-assisted vs. AI-automated distinction; human judgment is the scarce resource
- **Meaning as the new economy** — as AI makes content trivial, meaning becomes scarce
- **Essays as resistance** — writing essays is one of the last cognitive acts AI cannot replicate
- **Epistemic commons contamination** — attention economy consuming human cognitive capacity faster than it recovers
- **One-person business** — the $1M math, content waterfall strategy, personal brand as the business

## Core Frameworks

- **3 Pillars of Modern Business:** Brand, Content, Offer
- **Content Waterfall:** 1 newsletter/week → 1 YouTube/week → redistribute to all platforms
- **AI-Assisted vs. AI-Automated:** Human judgment + AI support vs. AI does everything
