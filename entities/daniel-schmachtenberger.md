# Daniel Schmachtenberger

Philosopher and systems thinker who coined the term "metacrisis" to describe the interconnected web of all global crises. One of the primary intellectual influences on the Human 3.0 framework.

## Key Contributions

### The Metacrisis Framework
Identified that our moment isn't defined by any single crisis but by the interconnected dynamics creating all crises simultaneously. Named the three generator functions driving civilizational collapse: rivalrous dynamics, substrate consumption, and exponential technology.

### The Third Attractor
Argues for the possibility of an anti-rivalrous, regenerative civilization as an alternative to catastrophic collapse or dystopian control. A stable equilibrium where success is structurally coupled — where your success requires my success.

### Generator Function Thinking
Popularized the concept that treating surface-level problems without addressing their deep structural causes is futile. Problems are symptoms; generator functions are the disease.

## Core Ideas in Human 3.0

- The assignment vs. agency crisis (most humans operate on assignments rather than self-generated purpose)
- The wisdom crisis ("we have the power of gods without the love and wisdom of gods")
- Anti-rivalrous civilization as the only stable path forward
- Civilizational consciousness must evolve as fast as technology
