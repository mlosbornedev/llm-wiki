## The $1 Million Math

**Monthly:** $1,000,000 ÷ 12 = **$83,333/month**
**Daily:** $83,333 ÷ 30 = **$2,777/day**

### Paths to $1M/year

| Strategy | Daily Requirement |
|----------|-------------------|
| Sell 18 × $150 products (course) | 18 sales/day |
| Sell 111 × $25 subscriptions | 111 sales/day |
| Land $5,000 clients | 1 every other day |
| Land $10,000 clients | 1 every 4 days |
| Combination approach | 1-2 clients/week + product sales |

### Traffic Requirements

At **2.5% conversion rate** on a landing page:
- Need **720 visitors/day** to make 18 sales on a $150 product
- This is achievable through social media (10-50K views/video or 500K-1M impressions/month)

> **Key insight:** "The barrier of entry is lower, but the skill cap is higher."

## The 3 Pillars of a Modern Business

1. **Brand** – Who you are, what you help people achieve, why people should care
2. **Content** – Ideas, opinions, teachings that attract people to your brand
3. **Offer** – Product, service, compelling landing page

---

## 1) Personal Brand Strategy

**Core principle:** You are the niche. Your beliefs, experiences, and interests create a unique point of view.

### Content Pillar Strategy
- **1 skill/interest** you plan to monetize
- **2 complementary interests** you can't shut up about
- Ground these in **pain points**, foundational topics, high-performing ideas
- Create a **1-2 sentence bio** that captures the most attractive parts

### Using AI for Brand Strategy
1. **Curate expert information** – Find YouTube videos/books from certified experts and chat with them via AI
2. **Turn sources into prompts** – Create prompts that interview you and generate a tailored strategy
3. **Advanced (optional):** Build a personal brand coach agent with competitor research

> **Note:** AI works best when you know what you want. Use expert sources, not just general AI opinions.

## 2) Content Creation (80/20 Framework)

### The Secret to Good Content
Content must solve a **pain point** or promise a **benefit**. You need to illustrate the "why" that makes people change behavior.

**The two mistakes beginners make:**
1. Having interesting ideas but failing to show why they matter to others
2. Not understanding how content connects to income (chasing platform monetization instead of building their own offers)

> "If you're the one who changes their behavior, they will remember you as the person who 'changed their life,' and they will start to trust you over any human or AI."

### Content Creation Process
1. **Write down ideas constantly** – Read books, listen to podcasts, reflect on life
2. **Identify pain point or benefit immediately** – Train yourself to ask "why does this matter?"
3. **Save structures you like** – Create a swipe folder; imitate structure/framework with your own ideas
4. **Practice until second nature**

### Content Ecosystem (Content Waterfall)
```
1 Newsletter/Week
        ↓
1 YouTube Video/Week (soft script from newsletter)
        ↓
├── Best posts → Reels/YT Shorts (read ideas, speak to camera)
├── Regular posts → Carousels for LinkedIn, IG, YouTube community
└── Platform distribution → Podcast apps
```

**Philosophy:** Focus on **few quality pieces** (1 long-form/week + several short-form/day) and redistribute to all platforms. Creating unique content for each platform decreases quality.

### Using AI for Content
- Use prompts to learn content writing principles (not just generate posts)
- **Do not post what AI generates directly** – use as first drafts only
- Always refine in your own voice

## 3) What You Sell

### The 3-Step Process
1. **Create a detailed customer avatar** – Reference this for all marketing materials
2. **Create your first offer** – Based on avatar and offer creation principles
3. **Turn both into a compelling landing page** – Show benefits, not just features

> **Warning:** "Most people create a boring product and then just illustrate what the product does on their landing page and wonder why nobody wants it."

## Key Insights on AI Usage

> "AI works best when you know what you want."

> "Agents are useful, but people don't understand what they're doing. They think an agent absolved them from learning."

> "AI, frankly, isn't good enough on its own. The person directing it is where the magic lies."

**AI is NOT for:**
- Doing everything for you
- Replacing learning
- Instant success without iteration

**AI IS for:**
- Learning faster (chat with expert content like a guru)
- Executing faster (drafts, frameworks, prompts)
- Iterating faster (testing variations)
