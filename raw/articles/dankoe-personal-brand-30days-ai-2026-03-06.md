## Overview

A tutorial video and practical guide for building a personal brand using AI as a **coaching/sparring tool** rather than having AI automate everything. Dan Koe emphasizes building authentic audience trust through human judgment.

---

## Core Philosophy

> *"The actionable strategies that you can actually use that probably aren't going to go viral, aren't going to do too well like they would on my main channel. But I feel like there are people who actually want to watch this stuff."*

**Key Distinction:**
- **AI-assisted** = Human judgment + AI support (recommended)
- **AI-automated** = AI does everything (not recommended)

> *"Audience trust is built on human judgment, not output volume."*

## Key Methodological Insights

1. **Flowchart vs. Content Pillars** — The flowchart approach is considered more practical than traditional "content pillars" which feels overused

2. **Using AI as a Business Coach** — Ask AI to identify blind spots and overlooked areas:
   > *"She's like my expensive business coach without the high price tag"*

3. **Claude vs. ChatGPT** — Community consensus favors Claude for this use case:
   > *"ChatGPT has LinkedIn energy"* vs. *"a lot calmer after speaking to Claude"*

4. **Claude Projects** — Recommended approach: Use Claude project files with custom instructions rather than custom GPTs

5. **Skills Creation** — Can create Claude skills from courses (e.g., 7-hour copywriting → skill) for various professions: personal trainers, nutritionists, financial planners, etc.
