# I'm Begging You to Write More Essays

**By Dan Koe** | *The best way to learn faster, think deeper, and save humanity*
**Source:** https://letters.thedankoe.com/p/im-begging-you-to-write-more-essays
**Date:** April 2, 2026

---

## Core Thesis

> The modern information environment is breaking our ability to think, and most people don't even notice. Essays might be one of the last forms of content that actually develops your capacity to make sense of reality.

Essays are one of the greatest tools to:
- Learn faster
- Think deeper
- Improve articulation of ideas and beliefs
- **Avoid being replaced by AI**

---

## Part I: The Epistemic Commons is Being Poisoned

### What is the "Epistemic Commons"?
Think of it as our **water source, but for information** — extremely important.

Most people consume news to stay "informed," but:
- Their lives aren't changing for the better
- People are becoming more jaded, polarized, and violent

### The Contamination Problem

> If the content you publish in public **hurts more than it helps**, and it is not counterbalanced by content that does help, our intellectual water source becomes contaminated.

**What you consume influences:**
- Identity
- Life trajectory and behavior
- Attention span
- Tolerance for complexity
- Ability to hold contradictions
- Capacity for nuance

### Key Question
> Does what you consume or create lead to a beneficial behavior change in yourself and others? Or are you unconsciously soaking in information that silently makes your life worse?

## Part II: Three Forces Breaking Civilization's Thinking

*Based on Daniel Schmachtenberger's framework on the "Metacrisis"*

### The Three Generator Functions

| Threat | Description | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Rivalrous Dynamics** | Win-lose games where one party's gain requires another's loss | Arms races, corporate competition, social media content competition, academic publishing |
| **Substrate Consumption** | Systems consuming their foundation faster than they regenerate | Depleting topsoil; **attention economy consuming human cognitive capacity** |
| **Exponential Technology** | Tools improving at accelerating rates, outpacing human wisdom | AI doubling in capacity, automated weapons, algorithms evolving faster than research |

### Three Possible Outcomes ("Attractors")

1. **Civilizational Collapse** — Nuclear war, unaligned AI, ecological destruction, engineered pandemics
2. **Dystopian Control** — Total surveillance, digital authoritarianism, elimination of individual agency
3. **The Third Attractor** (positive) — World where sense-making, shared understanding, and aligned incentives exist

### How This Plays Out Online

- Creators optimize for **engagement over transformation** (rivalrous dynamics)
- Algorithms measure clicks, watches, likes — not understanding
- Cognitive capacity (the "substrate") is consumed faster than it recovers

> AI itself isn't the issue. The issue is that it can **mimic what looks like real thinking without requiring any cognitive effort** from either the creator or consumer.

## Part III: Essays vs. Fast Content

### The Content Spectrum

| FAST CONTENT (Entropic) | SLOW CONTENT (Ordering) |
|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| Delivers conclusions without thought | Requires thinking |
| Pre-digested, algorithmic, often AI-generated | Genuine thinking in public |
| Feels informed but empty afterward | Generates meaning through engagement |

**Examples of fast content:** BuzzFeed listicles, rage tweets, AI summaries, hot takes, engagement-optimized threads, TikTok explainers that give the *feeling* of understanding in 30 seconds.

**Examples of slow content:** Essays, long-form conversations, certain books, thoughtful lectures.

### Why Essays Are Different

| Articles | Essays |
|----------|--------|
| Are answers | Are arguments |
| Package existing knowledge | Change the author's beliefs |
| Start with conclusion | Figure it out along the way |
| Inform or educate | Are an act of thinking |
| Communicate what's already there | Discover what isn't |

### Why AI Cannot Write Essays

> **The defining factor of an essay is that AI cannot write one.**

AI lacks:
- **Situated point of view** — no direct experience
- **Beliefs, biases, and emotions** that lead to thinking in a particular direction
- **The magic of surprise and discovery** — telling AI to share something novel destroys the chance of stumbling upon a genuine discovery

## Part IV: The Meaning Economy

### Why Meaning is the Scarcest Commodity

- **Pre-industrialization:** Gods in the sky were praised
- **Industrialization:** Productivity became the God
- **Today:** *More* is our God — more money, more information, more content

> We have more stuff and less purpose than ever.

### What is Meaning?

**Meaning = the experience of ordered consciousness**

When your consciousness is ordered — focused, purposeful, clear — you feel meaning. When it's disordered — scattered, anxious, overwhelmed — you feel emptiness.

### The Creator's Opportunity

> The people who can **create meaning** will be the most valuable people in the economy.

Writers, artists, philosophers, teachers — those who can help others order their consciousness — are the new scarcity.

---

## Summary

The essay is one of the last forms of content that develops thinking rather than just consuming it. As AI makes fast content trivial, the writers who can produce genuine essays — with a point of view, a felt sense of discovery, and the ability to help others make meaning — become more valuable, not less.
