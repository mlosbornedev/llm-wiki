## Core Thesis
> "If you're trying to get hired in AI, artifacts > resumes."

There are essentially infinite AI jobs right now — uncapped budgets at every company — yet employers can't find qualified talent.

## Key Statistics
- Accenture: Training 700,000 people on agentic AI
- ManpowerGroup (39,000 employers, 41 countries): AI skills are the hardest capability to find on Earth
- DataCamp (500 enterprise leaders): 40% of AI training is video courses; 23% doesn't translate to real work
- Accenture (2024): Only 26% of workers trained on how to collaborate with AI

## The Problem With Current AI Training
- Training is "taught at the wrong altitude" — "AI for Everyone" at top, deep ML at bottom, entire middle layer barely exists
- Teaches tools when employers hire for judgment
- Most courses show how to use ChatGPT; employers want: agentic evaluation mindset, including automated evals, simulation-based testing, regression frameworks, and metrics design
- Certifications are "mostly badges from Saturday afternoon lectures that hiring managers have been ignoring for years"

## The K-Shaped Split
"Infinite jobs" + "I can't get hired" = both true simultaneously due to the K-shaped split in the AI labor market.
