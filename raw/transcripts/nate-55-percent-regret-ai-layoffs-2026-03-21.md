## Core Thesis
AI agents excel at individual tasks but fail at entire jobs due to memory limitations and lack of institutional context. 55% of employers now regret AI-driven workforce reductions.

## The Memory Gap
- Average software job tenure: 18 months – 2 years
- Average AI agent run: ~2 hours
- People who hold institutional context: 4, 5, 6, 7+ years

People who truly understand a business stay for years. AI agents reset with every session and lack awareness of what they don't know.

## The Grigorev Incident (Case Study)
Alexey Grigorev's AI coding agent wiped his production database — 1.9 million rows of student data, gone in seconds. The backups disappeared too. The agent never made a technical error. Every action was locally correct. The agent couldn't distinguish real infrastructure from temporary copies. That knowledge existed only in the engineer's head.

## The Paradox: More Powerful = More Dangerous
- Mediocre tool that fails obviously → Annoying
- Powerful tool that fails silently → Dangerous

## Key Argument
> "The best tools we have for managing agent risk are human brains and human brains crafting evaluations. Not better prompts. Not bigger context windows. Human judgment about what matters, what's fragile, and what the AI doesn't know it doesn't know."
