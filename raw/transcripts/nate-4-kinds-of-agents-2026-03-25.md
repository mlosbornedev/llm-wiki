## Core Thesis
> "The word 'agent' has become the 'cloud' of 2026. Everyone uses it. Almost nobody means the same thing."

There are four distinct architectures that most people lump together as "agents": coding harnesses, dark factories, auto research, and orchestration frameworks — "about as much in common as a forklift and a bicycle."

## Market Context
- 2025 AI Agent Market: ~$8 billion
- 2030 Projected Market: >$50 billion

## The Four Architectures
1. **Coding harnesses** — For software development tasks
2. **Dark factories** — Automated manufacturing/production systems
3. **Auto research** — Research and information synthesis
4. **Orchestration frameworks** — Coordinating multiple agents/tasks

## Common Mistakes
- Dark factory → Writing a novel (wrong tool for creative work)
- CrewAI → Simple coding problems (needed single agent + test suite)
- Auto research loop → Writing new software (like "pointing a profiler at an empty file")

## The One-Question Test
A diagnostic question that cuts through ambiguity — works for ICs choosing tools or CTOs setting strategy.

## Operating Principles
- Decomposition
- Specification-as-code
- Metric-plus-guardrail
- Handoff contracts

## Three Diagnostic Prompts
1. Classifies problems into the right architecture
2. Pressure-tests readiness
3. Catches mismatches before wasting a quarter

## Key Quote
> "The taxonomy matters because the fix for each problem lives in a different place than most people are looking."
