## Core Thesis
> "Apple isn't behind. Apple is playing a different game. And if the evidence is right, it's the game that matters more."

Apple is building an agentic runtime baked into the operating system itself — not a chatbot. This positions Apple as the chokepoint between users, AI agents, and every application on the device.

## The Strategic Distinction
| Approach | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Model Race | Competing to build the best LLM |
| Platform Play | Building the layer every model must pass through |

> "The model race is a content play. The runtime is a platform play. Apple has never lost a platform play."

## Evidence Chain: Four Interlocking Signals

### 1. The Siri Overhaul (WWDC June 8, 2026)
Mark Gurman published detailed leaks. "The headline everyone will write: Siri finally becomes a chatbot. The chatbot is the skin. The skeleton is far more consequential."

### 2. MCP Integration into iOS
Apple is wiring the Model Context Protocol (the industry's standard agent protocol) directly into iOS.

### 3. Google's Asymmetric Deal
The Gemini deal that appears to be a win-win contains structural asymmetry that favors the company controlling the model.

### 4. Apple's Existing Distribution Advantage
1.5+ billion iPhones in pockets of highest-spending consumers on Earth. OpenAI is reportedly paying billions to Jony Ive to build hardware for distribution Apple already has.
