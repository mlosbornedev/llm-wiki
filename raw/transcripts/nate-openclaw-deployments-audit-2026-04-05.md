## The Middleware Trap
Organizations deploy an intelligent layer on top of existing systems, celebrate the speed, and never examine whether they've automated their dysfunction.

The agent doesn't fix:
- A broken data model
- An unmapped workflow
- A misaligned org structure

What it actually does: Inherits all three and executes on them at machine speed, with full confidence, to every downstream system at once.

## Three Layers of Compounding Risk
| Layer | Surface Timeline | Reversibility |
|-------|-------------------|---------------|
| Dirty data | Earliest | Most reversible |
| Unmapped workflows | Medium-term | Moderate difficulty |
| Unredesigned orgs | Longest | Hardest to unwind |

## Key Insight
**Security is described as a "symptom"** — the most dangerous OpenClaw vulnerabilities are organizational authority vacuums that no patch can fix.
