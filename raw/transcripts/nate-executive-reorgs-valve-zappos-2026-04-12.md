## April 14 Update: Three Jobs Inside Every Manager

44% of U.S. workers say their employer cut at least one layer of management in the past year (Korn Ferry).

Management is a bundle of three distinct functions:

| Function | Definition | Automation Timeline |
|----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Routing** | Moving information between people and teams | **Automatable NOW** |
| **Sensemaking** | Interpreting what information means for specific decisions | **18-36 months out** |
| **Accountability** | Telling people whether they're on track and helping them grow | **May NEVER be fully automatable** |

### The Misdiagnosis Problem

"The symptoms of a sensemaking vacuum — six people read the same strategy memo, propose six incompatible responses, and nobody in the room has the authority or context to resolve it — look exactly like a communication problem."

Companies respond by adding more alignment meetings, another all-hands, a cross-functional Slack channel. This is routing — the one function AI already handles.

### The Accountability Gap

Your best mid-tenure people stop waiting for feedback that isn't coming and start interviewing. By the time the attrition data surfaces, you've lost the people hardest to replace.

### The Sequence That Works

1. Replace routing (do this first — AI handles it)
2. Protect feedback (second — preserve accountability)
3. Concentrate sensemaking (third — this is the hard part)

"Reversing any two produces a specific, predictable disaster."

### The Flat-Org Graveyard

Companies that removed all three functions at once:
- Valve
- Zappos
- Medium
- GitHub

### Current Case Studies

- **Meta:** Stripped out entire layers, stock nearly tripled, model being studied and emulated
- **Shopify:** Teams must prove AI couldn't do the job before approving a hire
- **Kimi, Block, Meta:** Each decomposing the management bundle differently — what's working, what's breaking, what each model trades away

### Diagnosis vs. Misdiagnosis

| If You Lost... | Then... | Don't Do... |
|----------------|---------|-------------|
| **Routing** | Use AI tools | Add more meetings |
| **Sensemaking** | Concentrate decision authority | Create Slack channels |
| **Accountability** | Protect feedback loops | Assume people self-manage |

"The companies that diagnose which function they actually lost will fix this in a quarter. The ones that keep treating it as a communication problem will re-add layers within two years and call it 'maturing the organization.'"
