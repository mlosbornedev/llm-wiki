## Core Thesis
4:1 ratio for agent deployment problems:
- 4 problems = well-understood engineering with 50 years of precedent → your team can solve
- 1 problem = domain expertise requiring specialized help → worth paying consultants

> "The agent deployment problems that everyone frames as unprecedented are, with one critical exception, well-understood engineering with fifty years of precedent."

## The Two Competing Stories

### Story 1: "Build Your Own" (Nvidia/Open Source)
- Monday at GTC: Jensen Huang released NeMoClaw — an open-source agent security stack
- Huang told every company in the world to build their own agent infrastructure
- This is Nvidia's theory: agent deployment is a solvable engineering problem

### Story 2: "You Need Help" (OpenAI/Consulting Firms)
- Three weeks earlier: OpenAI signed multi-year partnerships with McKinsey, BCG, Accenture, and Capgemini
- Anthropic signed similar deals with Accenture and Deloitte
- OpenAI's framing: "The limiting factor isn't model intelligence — it's how agents are built and run in organizations"
