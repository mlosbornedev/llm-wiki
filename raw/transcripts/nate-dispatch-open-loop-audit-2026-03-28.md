## Core News
Anthropic shipped Dispatch and computer use — enabling users to delegate to AI agents via text from anywhere (even mobile devices). The AI can take over desktops, open apps, click through screens, navigate tools without APIs, and deliver finished work.

## The Core Problem
The #1 mistake: Pointing Dispatch at simulated work — triage reports, email summaries, proactive briefings that "sound important but are really just another document to read."

> "The agent looks busy. Your life doesn't change."

## Key Distinction
Does the work land on your desk or leave it?

| Simulated Work (Bad) | Real Delegation (Good) |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| Creates more documents to read | Work disappears from your desk |
| Tasks pile up | Commitments met automatically |
| Needs review/editing | Delivered finished |
