## Core Thesis
A single AI agent triggered a quarter-trillion-dollar selloff in enterprise software stocks. It's a research preview that stops working when your laptop goes to sleep.

> "Code has a test suite. A strategy memo doesn't compile. When an outcome agent drafts a report from your scattered notes, nothing tells it whether it captured the right insights or left out the thing that mattered most."

## Why Code Worked First
| Environment | Feedback Type | Agent Reliability |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Software/Code | Automated (tests compile, pass/fail) | High |
| Knowledge Work | Human-dependent (you are the only feedback) | Low |

## The Three Questions Framework
Tools reviewed: Lindy, Sauna, Google Opal, Obvious

## Key Principles for Evaluating AI Agents
1. **Memory Architecture** — How the agent stores and retrieves context
2. **Inspectable Surfaces** — Can you verify what the agent did and why?
3. **Compounding Context** — Does the agent improve over time with your feedback?

## Key Takeaway
> "Software that does the work instead of helping you do the work. Outcomes, not answers."

The best-performing tool scored 1 out of 4 on the evaluation framework.
