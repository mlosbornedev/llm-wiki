## Summary

The world runs on inefficiency/arbitrage gaps. AI is closing them on the timescale of model releases, not decades. Every gap that closes opens three new ones — this is "micro-turbulence," a permanent condition of rolling disruption.

Key example: Polymarket bot that turned $313 into ~$438K in one month, 98% win rate across 6,615 trades. It exploited a speed gap — Polymarket's short-duration crypto contracts updated slower than spot exchanges. Average arbitrage windows shrank from 12.3 seconds (2024) to 2.7 seconds (early 2026). 92.4% of Polymarket wallets lost money.

**Five gap taxonomy:**
1. Speed gaps — one system updates slower than reality (Polymarket case)
2. Reasoning gaps — interpretation of public information faster/more consistently
3. Fragmentation gaps — same thing priced differently in different places; aggregation
4. Discipline gaps — execution consistency AI enforces that humans can't maintain
5. Knowledge asymmetry gaps — the big one; labor arbitrage → intelligence arbitrage

**The compression:** democratized AI access = appearance of democratized advantage. 92.4% lose money. The gap is "bolted AI onto existing process" vs "rebuilt process around what AI makes possible."

**The rotation:** disruption never settles. Each model release opens new gaps, each compressing faster than the last. Markets repriced within hours of Mythos leak in March 2026. No equilibrium — only the next rotation.

**Three diagnostic questions for any industry:**
1. What inefficiency is this built on?
2. How fast can AI close it?
3. What new gap does the closure create?

New gap is always upstream: judgment, taste, relationships, system-level thinking — further from production/execution/information retrieval.
