# Meaning Economy

The emerging thesis that as AI makes productivity and content creation trivial, **meaning becomes the scarce commodity** — and those who can create meaning (writers, artists, philosophers, teachers) become more valuable, not less.

## Core Thesis

**Meaning = the experience of ordered consciousness.** When your consciousness is ordered — focused, purposeful, clear — you feel meaning. When it's disordered — scattered, anxious, overwhelmed — you feel emptiness.

Three historical "gods" as societies organized around:
1. **Pre-industrialization:** Gods in the sky
2. **Industrialization:** Productivity
3. **Today:** *More* — more money, more information, more content

> We have more stuff and less purpose than ever.

## The Creator's Opportunity

The people who can **create meaning** will be the most valuable people in the economy. Writers, artists, philosophers, teachers — those who can help others order their consciousness — are the new scarcity.
