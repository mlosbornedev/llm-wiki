# AI-Assisted vs AI-Automated

A critical distinction in how to use AI tools: **AI-assisted** (human judgment + AI support) vs **AI-automated** (AI does everything).

## The Core Distinction

| | **AI-Assisted** | **AI-Automated** |
|--|----------------|-----------------|
| **What it is** | Human judgment drives; AI supports execution | AI does the work; human is absent |
| **Result** | Quality + authenticity + learning | Volume; often generic; no skill development |
| **Audience trust** | Built on human judgment | Eroded by output volume |
| **Recommended?** | Yes | No |

> "Audience trust is built on human judgment, not output volume." — Dan Koe

## What AI Cannot Replace

AI lacks:
- **Situated point of view** — no direct experience of the world
- **Beliefs, biases, and emotions** that give thinking a particular direction
- **The magic of surprise and discovery** — genuine insight can't be anticipated

## The Human Skill That Matters More

> "AI works best when you know what you want."

The limiting factor is not AI capability — it's the human's ability to:
- Curate expert sources
- Direct the AI with clear intent
- Refine and apply judgment to outputs
- Know what to ask for

## Practical Applications

- **AI as business coach/sparring partner** — ask AI to identify blind spots ("She's like my expensive business coach without the high price tag")
- **AI for drafts and frameworks** — first drafts, not final content
- **AI for learning** — chat with expert content, not just generate posts
- **Never post what AI generates directly** — always refine in your own voice
