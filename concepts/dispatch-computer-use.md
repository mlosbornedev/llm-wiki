# Dispatch / Computer Use

Anthropic's Dispatch and computer use capabilities — enabling AI agents to take over desktops, open apps, click through screens, and navigate tools without APIs.

## What It Does
You text Claude from your phone. It takes over your desktop. Opens apps. Clicks through screens. Navigates tools with no API. Delivers finished work while you're away.

Not a summary to review. Not a draft to edit. The finished work, handled.

## The #1 Mistake
**Pointing Dispatch at simulated work:**
- Triage reports
- Email summaries
- Proactive briefings that "sound important but are really just another document to read"

> "The agent looks busy. Your life doesn't change."

## The Key Distinction
Does the work land on your desk or leave it?

| Simulated Work | Real Delegation |
|---------------|----------------|
| Creates more documents to read | Work disappears from your desk |
| Tasks pile up | Commitments met automatically |
| Needs review/editing | Delivered finished |

## Four Prompts Included
1. **Open loop audit prompt** — separates real delegation from simulated work
2. Ready-to-paste briefs for Dispatch handoffs
3. Recurring automations
4. Decision research that fights confirmation bias
