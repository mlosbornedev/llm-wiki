# Anti-Vision Principle

Your anti-vision — the life you *don't* want — often provides clearer initial direction than positive vision. Start by identifying what you absolutely refuse to accept, then work backward to solutions.

## Why It Works

When positive vision is abstract or overwhelming, the negative is concrete and visceral. "I don't want to be the person who wakes up at 60 with no savings, bad health, and estranged kids" is more actionable than "I want to live my best life."

The anti-vision also reveals constraints clearly. Most people haven't articulated what they refuse — and therefore drift into it. Clarity about what you refuse is the minimum requirement for avoiding it.

## The Framework

**Mind Anti-Vision:** What mental states do you absolutely refuse? What cognitive limitations frustrate you most? What thinking patterns in others disgust you? What intellectual stagnation terrifies you you?

**Body Anti-Vision:** What physical state do you refuse? What health problems terrify you? What physical limitations anger you? What aging pattern do you reject?

**Spirit Anti-Vision:** What relationship dynamics do you absolutely refuse to repeat? What meaningless existence do you reject? What spiritual emptiness haunts you? What isolation do you fear?

**Vocation Anti-Vision:** What work situation makes you feel dead inside? What financial state do you refuse to accept? What professional identity repulses you? What legacy would shame you?

## Morning Writing Practice

The 30-minute morning writing stack:
1. **5 min:** Anti-vision clarity — what you refuse today
2. **10 min:** Problem identification and solution design
3. **10 min:** Value creation — who you can help today
4. **5 min:** Public sharing — post, message, or article

## When to Use

Anti-vision is the entry point, not the destination. Once you know what you refuse, you can ask: "Given what I refuse, what do I actually want?" The positive vision emerges from the negative constraint.
