# Metacrisis

Daniel Schmachtenberger's term for our moment: not one crisis but the interconnected web of all global crises, driven by three structural generator functions. Without addressing these generators, treating symptoms just mops water while the faucet runs.

## The Three Generator Functions

### 1. Rivalrous Dynamics
Win-lose games where one party's gain requires another's loss. Creates destructive races — arms races, price wars, attention competition.

Examples: Nations spending trillions on weapons, companies externalizing costs, social media addiction algorithms, academic data-hoarding.

### 2. Substrate Consumption
Complex systems consuming their own foundations faster than those foundations regenerate. The "substrate" = what systems need to exist: topsoil, attention, trust, cognitive capacity.

Examples: Industrial agriculture depleting topsoil, attention economy consuming cognition, financial extraction draining the real economy.

### 3. Exponential Technology
Tools that improve themselves at accelerating rates, outpacing wisdom and institutions. Each generation enables the next, creating exponential curves.

Examples: AI doubling every few months, CRISPR before consequences understood, social algorithms evolving faster than research.

## The Convergence Crisis

These three interact catastrophically:

- Rivalrous + Exponential = Arms races with existential weapons
- Substrate consumption + Rivalrous = Tragedy of the commons at planetary scale
- Exponential + Substrate consumption = Accelerating extraction until collapse

## The Two Attractors

Without fundamental change, dynamics push toward:

1. **Catastrophic Collapse:** Ecological breakdown, nuclear war, unaligned AI, bioweapons, cascading infrastructure failure
2. **Dystopian Control:** Total surveillance, digital authoritarianism, permanent inequality, loss of human agency to AI

## The Third Option: Anti-Rivalrous Civilization

Anti-rivalrous dynamics go beyond "win-win." In anti-rivalrous systems, your success *literally requires* my success — structural coupling for mutual thriving.

Examples: Organs in a body, parent-child relationships, blockchain networks (each node adds security), regenerative agriculture (each farm improves neighbors' conditions).

The window for creating this third attractor is rapidly narrowing.

## Why Individual Development Matters

We cannot build anti-rivalrous systems with rivalrous minds. Human 3.0 development is civilizational work:

- Collective intelligence requires individually sovereign thinkers
- Complex global problems require systemic thinkers
- The power of technology demands proportional wisdom
- Partial development perpetuates the very dynamics creating the crisis

## The Assignment vs. Agency Crisis

Most of humanity operates on *assignments* — goals given by schools, employers, society — rather than self-generated purpose. This creates low-agency individuals who perpetuate rivalrous systems. When humans follow assignments rather than creating their own path, they become tools that can be replaced and rivalrous actors competing for programmed goals.
