# Reorg Three Functions

The insight that management layers actually bundle three distinct functions, each on a completely different automation timeline — and removing all three simultaneously is the root cause of every famous flat-org failure.

## The Three Functions

| Function | What It Does | Automation Timeline | Failure When Removed |
|----------|-------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Routing** | Moving information between people/teams | Automatable NOW | Information bottlenecks; silos |
| **Sensemaking** | Interpreting what information means for decisions | 18–36 months out | Six people read the same memo, propose six incompatible responses |
| **Accountability** | Telling people if they're on track, helping them grow | May NEVER be fully automatable | Best mid-tenure people leave |

## The Flat-Org Graveyard

Companies that removed all three at once all hit the same wall:
- Valve
- Zappos
- Medium
- GitHub

The pattern repeats with consistency — treat it as a structural finding, not a cautionary tale.

## The Misdiagnosis Trap

Adding more meetings, all-hands, Slack channels only adds **routing** (the one AI already handles) while sensemaking remains broken.

"The symptoms of a sensemaking vacuum look exactly like a communication problem." Companies respond by solving routing — spending leadership attention doing what software does better, while the actual broken function goes unaddressed.

## The Sequence That Works

1. Replace routing → AI handles information flow now
2. Protect feedback → Maintain accountability functions
3. Concentrate sensemaking → Centralize decision interpretation

Reversing any two steps produces a specific, predictable disaster.

## Diagnosis vs. Misdiagnosis

| If You Lost... | Then... | Don't Do... |
|----------------|---------|-------------|
| **Routing** | Use AI tools | Add more meetings |
| **Sensemaking** | Concentrate decision authority | Create Slack channels |
| **Accountability** | Protect feedback loops | Assume people self-manage |

## Current Case Studies (April 2026)

- **Meta:** Stripped out entire layers, stock nearly tripled, model being studied and emulated
- **Shopify:** Teams must prove AI couldn't do the job before approving a hire (conservative/cautious)
- **Kimi, Block:** Each decomposing the management bundle differently — what's working, what's breaking, what each model trades away

## Key Quote

> "Your company removed a management layer. It was actually three layers in a trench coat, and only one of them was ready to go."
