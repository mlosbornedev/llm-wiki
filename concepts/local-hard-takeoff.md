# Local Hard Takeoff

A "Local Hard Takeoff" is a domain-specific intelligence explosion. Unlike the global AI safety "hard takeoff" (general intelligence explosion), this is bounded to a specific business system, metric, and sandbox.

## Characteristics
- **Steep Trajectory:** Improvements compound much faster than a human-led organization can track.
- **Bounded Scope:** The agent becomes world-class at one specific thing (e.g., a pricing engine, fraud detection, or a specific agent harness) without necessarily generalizing.
- **Competitive Asymmetry:** Organizations that can define "better" (metrics) and build the infrastructure (eval harnesses) pull away from those relying on human-speed quarterly planning cycles.

## Prerequisites for Local Takeoff
1. **Scorable Metrics:** Clear definitions of success.
2. **Eval Harnesses:** Sandboxed environments for running hundreds of experiments.
3. **Trace Infrastructure:** Detailed reasoning trajectories (not just outcomes) are required for the meta-agent to make surgical edits rather than random mutations.
